OSwind / research & delivery notebook / 22 Oct 2026

A memory for your Mac.
A measurable path to parity.

Screen history, conversations, local intelligence and useful recall. This report compares 16 relevant products and projects, then turns their capabilities into an explicit OSwind delivery ledger.

Research snapshot: primary publisher documentation. Feature descriptions are vendor claims unless a test is explicitly identified. OSwind is an initial implementation; feature-complete and top-three claims are not established.

There are three different products hiding in this category.

Replay what you saw

Original Rewind, ScreenMemory, Rewind Desktop, rem and Retrace emphasize screenshots/video, OCR, a visual timeline and source recovery.

Remember what you worked on

Screenpipe, Pieces, Littlebird, TimeScroll and text-first Minne add contextual retrieval, assistants, recaps, routines and integrations. Local capture and local inference are separate decisions.

Remember what was said

Limitless, Omi and Granola prioritize speech, speaker context, notes and actions. Full desktop parity requires both visual and audio evidence.

OSwind’s product direction: native Mac capture and evidence browsing; local inference as a first-class path; BYOK without lock-in; explicit data controls; performance measured across active capture, idle, retrieval and model loading. Native implementation helps but is not proof of better power use.

Build priorities that move the whole product forward

  1. Close correctness boundaries: lock/startup/pause, privacy context across in-flight work, raw-media handling, transactional imports, cancellation and storage pressure. Verify live browser/audio behavior.
  2. Make recall trustworthy: extend the delivered local GPU embeddings with multimodal retrieval, natural-language dates, query highlighting, evidence citations and a diverse gold retrieval set. Keep keyword mode available.
  3. Finish daily-use workflows: extend delivered durable chats, meeting sessions, short audio flush, diarization, timed pause, scheduled routines, Markdown/media exports and migration.
  4. Prove sustained performance: 10k/100k/1m retrieval scaling, capture-content benchmarks, all-night soak, disk-growth and physical power measurements. Add ANN and hardware video compression only with evidence.
  5. Close polish/security parity: vault/Touch ID, accessibility/focus review, browser and older-macOS matrix, notarized installer, crash recovery, update channel and repeatable competitor runs.

Personal sync, team controls, enterprise governance and cross-platform clients remain visible future scope. They do not belong in a single-user macOS v0 completion claim.

Method and limits

Reviewed on 22 October 2026, America/Los_Angeles. Sources are linked in each product card. No vendor marketing benchmark was adopted as an independent measurement. Product code is independently implemented. An isolated comparison harness compiles pinned MIT rem database methods in ignored build folders, retaining its license; no rem source is embedded in the product. License names describe reviewed repositories and do not constitute a compatibility audit.

Original Rewind/Limitless is sunset for new desktop capture, while rewind.app is an unrelated current offering. Two unrelated projects are named Retrace. Pensieve was previously named Memos. These naming collisions matter when evaluating downloads and feature claims.

Publisher pages can disagree. Screenpipe’s current onboarding table is used for annual plans; its commercial-source requirements are preserved. Pieces’ current page supersedes older free-tier summaries. Free-tier inconsistencies on ScreenMemory/Omi are flagged. Enterprise compliance statements are publisher claims, not reviewed certifications.

Local-only means all selected processing remains local: OCR, transcription, embeddings, inference, storage, sync and connected-client behavior must each be checked. OSwind’s optional BYOK path intentionally sends selected text to the configured provider. Pattern-based redaction is incomplete protection.

Native sample layouts and report filtering were visually reviewed; targeted synthetic capture passed while unlocked. An unlocked native session verified model preparation, strategy selection and semantic search. Broader live setup, shortcut retesting, automatic foreground/browser transitions and audio remain open gates. This report and its machine-readable companion are maintained project artifacts, not a completion certificate.
