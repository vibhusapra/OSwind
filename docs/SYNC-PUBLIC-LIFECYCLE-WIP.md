## Two-Mac setup and erase review

Pair both Macs before delivery. On the first Mac, create a new sync folder; on the second, select the same locally available folder through its provider. The app stores a bounded local bookmark, verifies the signed object space and transfers ownership into a tracked session. A saved delivery reservation hides unsupported pairing retirement and membership edits even after disconnect. Reconnect is explicit from Settings; relaunch does not silently start delivery. The preview does not offer a third device until certificate refresh is implemented.

After a local erase, reconnect and review the session-bound saved group decision. Approval names the admitted Macs and explains that unsynced peer history can be removed when the control is observed. Cancel keeps sync waiting. Encrypted provider archives, version history and independent backups are not purged. A received approved decision uses the original verified session and needs no second approval on that Mac. Capture choices remain separate; completed erasure reopens quietly.
